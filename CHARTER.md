# Charter

OTP form assistant helps a person complete a structured form through typed
or spoken conversation. It provides form contracts, drafts with undo and
redo, assistant tools, review, Phoenix LiveView components, and optional
supervised voice sessions.

Hosts supply authorization, persistence, model
requests, and authenticated transports. The package does not own business
schemas, telephony, customer records, credentials, or deployment policy.

## Origin
